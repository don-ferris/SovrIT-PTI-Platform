# SovrIT Canon

## Section 1 — Identity

### What is SovrIT?

SovrIT (Sovereign Information Technology) is a Personal Technology
Infrastructure (PTI).

It is not a homelab.

It is not a collection of self-hosted services.

It is not a NAS.

It is not a cloud replacement.

SovrIT is a unified, encrypted, geographically distributed,
highly-available Personal Technology Infrastructure designed to preserve
the digital sovereignty of the household it serves.

---

## Mission

SovrIT replaces dependence on third-party cloud providers with an
integrated, local-first platform operated under the direct custodianship
of the household.

Its mission is to preserve:

- Privacy
- Security
- Resilience
- Long-term continuity
- Ownership of data
- Ownership of metadata
- Operational independence

---

## Digital Sovereignty

Within SovrIT, digital sovereignty means preserving the household's
ability to own, control, protect, recover, and privately use every
essential digital capability without requiring trust in third-party
service providers.

Digital sovereignty includes:

- Local ownership of encryption keys.
- Freedom from vendor lock-in.
- Local-first AI.
- Local-first communications.
- Local-first smart-home functionality.
- Local-first security.
- Local-first surveillance.
- Long-term archival.
- Complete recoverability.
- Private remote access.
- Confidentiality of both data and metadata.

---

## Long-Term Vision

SovrIT is intended to remain viable for decades.

Its continued operation should not depend upon:

- Any cloud provider.
- Any software project.
- Any vendor.
- Any maintainer.
- Any repository.
- Any administrator.
- Any implementation technology.

Capabilities persist.

Implementations evolve.

---

## Platform Continuity

Every upstream dependency should be considered temporary.

Where practical:

- Upstream repositories are forked.
- Changes are evaluated before adoption.
- Minor updates may be selectively merged.
- Major releases receive increased scrutiny.
- Unsuitable implementations are replaced rather than tolerated.

Forking is an insurance policy against platform extinction.

---

## Automation Philosophy

Automation exists to preserve continuity.

AI capabilities should:

- Assist users.
- Protect the platform.
- Detect physical threats.
- Detect network threats.
- Mitigate threats where appropriate.
- Monitor upstream ecosystems.
- Evaluate software changes.
- Maintain documentation.
- Preserve platform integrity.
- Preserve operational continuity.
- Perform autonomous resiliency operations.
- Escalate only when uncertainty, elevated risk, or architectural
  judgment exceeds delegated authority.

---

## Enduring Identity

SovrIT is not the collection of software from which it is built.

SovrIT is the enduring capability to provide sovereign personal
technology services, regardless of how those capabilities are
implemented over time.
