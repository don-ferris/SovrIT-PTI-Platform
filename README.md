# SovrIT  
**Personal Information Technology Platform**

SovrIT is a sovereign, self‑hosted personal infrastructure platform designed to return control of your digital life to you. It replaces dependence on third‑party cloud services with a modular ecosystem you operate yourself—private by default, transparent by design, and understandable and maintainable by non-technical people in your household.

---

## 🌱 Mission

Most personal data today lives inside third‑party clouds you do not control. SovrIT reverses that pattern by giving you ownership of your identity, communication, storage, automation, and daily digital routines.

Where external services are unavoidable—such as mobile carriers—SovrIT provides sovereign layers (self‑hosted VPN, encrypted communication, data‑only mobile workflows) to minimize exposure and protect confidentiality.

Your data should belong to you, not to the companies that move and store it.

---

## 🧩 SovrIT Ecosystem

SovrIT is composed of focused, interoperable modules. Each lives in its own repository:

- **SovrIT Core** — architecture, design philosophy, and guiding principles  
- **SovrIT Identity** — authentication, MFA, and account stewardship  
- **SovrIT Vault** — secrets, passwords, and encrypted storage  
- **SovrIT Mesh** — networking, VPN, and secure remote access  
- **SovrIT Ledger** — budgeting, financial data, and long‑term records  
- **SovrIT Presence** — home automation, sensors, and daily routines  
- **SovrIT Docs** — household documentation and manuals  
- **SovrIT Relay** — notifications and cross‑system messaging  
- **SovrIT Archive** — backups, snapshots, and long‑term preservation  
- **SovrIT Comms** — sovereign voice, text, and messaging for mobile devices  

Each module follows the principles defined in **SovrIT Core**.
