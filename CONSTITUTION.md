# SovrIT Constitution

> _"SovrIT is not the collection of software from which it is built.
> SovrIT is the enduring capability to provide sovereign personal
> technology services, regardless of how those capabilities are
> implemented over time."_

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the SovrIT Personal Technology Infrastructure (PTI) is to
preserve the digital sovereignty of the household it serves through a
secure, resilient, local-first, self-hosted technology ecosystem.

This Constitution defines the enduring principles that govern every
architectural, operational, and implementation decision within SovrIT.

Implementations may change.

Capabilities endure.

---

# Constitutional Principles

## 1. Digital Sovereignty

The user (or household) is the ultimate custodian of its digital life.

Digital sovereignty includes ownership, control, confidentiality,
availability, resilience, and long-term stewardship of:

- Data
- Metadata
- Identity
- Communications
- Knowledge
- Automation
- AI interactions
- Digital services

---

## 2. Capability Primacy

SovrIT defines permanence in terms of capabilities — not implementations.

Software projects...

Operating systems...

Programming languages...

Protocols...

Hardware...

Container technologies...

Vendors...

...are all replaceable.

The capabilities they provide constitute the enduring identity of the
platform.

---

## 3. Platform Continuity

No individual software project, vendor, maintainer, repository,
technology, administrator, or hardware platform shall become
indispensable.

Platform continuity always takes precedence over implementation loyalty.

Forking upstream projects exists primarily as an insurance policy against
platform extinction.

---

## 4. Third-Party Custodianship

Whenever a practical self-hosted alternative exists that preserves both
data confidentiality and metadata confidentiality without materially
compromising SovrIT's objectives, it shall be preferred over a
third-party hosted service—even when the self-hosted solution provides
fewer features or conveniences.

---

## 5. Household Continuity

The platform shall remain operable during the temporary or permanent
absence of its primary administrator.

Routine operations should be automated.

Human intervention should be exceptional.

When human intervention becomes necessary, it should be executable by
non-experts using accurate, continuously maintained documentation.

---

## 6. Delegated Automation

Automation exists to reduce operational burden while preserving human
authority.

Autonomous agents are expected to operate independently within clearly
defined operational boundaries.

Routine maintenance, monitoring, documentation, resiliency operations,
and service recovery should occur without human involvement whenever the
appropriate response is well understood.

Operations involving uncertainty, irreversible consequences,
architectural change, security ambiguity, or elevated risk shall require
human review or explicit authorization.

---

# Constitutional Question

Every significant architectural decision should answer:

> Does this strengthen or weaken the long-term continuity of the
> Personal Technology Infrastructure?
